Rental Terms & Rules

⛽ Full-to-Full Fuel Policy

The vehicle will be turned over to you with a full tank of fuel. It must be returned with a full tank to avoid refueling penalties and surcharges.

🪪 Valid Identification

Renters must physically present a valid Professional or Non-Professional Driver's License and one additional government-issued ID upon pickup.

💵 Security & Reservation

A fixed reservation fee via GCash is required to block off your selected dates. The remaining balance of the rental fee is payable upon pickup.

⏰ Late Return Penalty

Vehicles returned past the agreed drop-off time will incur a strict hourly penalty fee. Please contact us in advance if you need a rental extension.
